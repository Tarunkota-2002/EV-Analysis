## India EV Adoption & Infrastructure Analytics Dashboard !


### Project Overview

This Power BI project provides a comprehensive analysis of Electric Vehicle (EV) adoption trends across India. The dashboard helps stakeholders understand state-wise EV growth, manufacturer performance, charging infrastructure readiness, market share, and future adoption trends through forecasting and advanced analytics.

The solution follows a real-world business intelligence approach, including data cleaning, dimensional modeling, DAX calculations, KPI development, and interactive dashboard design.

---

### Business Problem

As EV adoption accelerates across India, government agencies, manufacturers, and investors need data-driven insights to:

* Monitor EV adoption growth
* Identify high-growth states
* Evaluate charging infrastructure readiness
* Analyze manufacturer market share
* Forecast future EV demand
* Support strategic expansion decisions

---

### Key Objectives

* Analyze state-wise EV registrations
* Measure Year-over-Year (YoY) growth
* Evaluate EV charging infrastructure coverage
* Compare manufacturer performance and market share
* Identify emerging EV markets
* Forecast future EV adoption trends
